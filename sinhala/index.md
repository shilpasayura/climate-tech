# Shilpa Sayura: ClimateTech STEM Education
### Empowering the next generation to solve climate challenges through technology.

Welcome to the **ClimateTech** project repository. This initiative, supported by the **Asia Pacific Vision Network (APVN)**, integrates Coding, IoT, and AI to help students understand and mitigate climate change through practical, hands-on engineering.

---

## 🌍 Our Mission
We bridge the gap between digital literacy and environmental stewardship. By providing structured learning pathways, we enable students to build real-world solutions for **Smart Agriculture**, **Disaster Preparedness**, and **Environmental Monitoring**.


## 🎓 Educational Pathways
Our curriculum is tailored to ensure age-appropriate technical growth:

| Grade Level | Core Technology | Primary Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grades 6–8** | Scratch & Micro:bit | Visual Coding & Basic Sensing |
| **Grade 9** | Arduino | Electronic Circuits & Prototyping |
| **Grade 10+** | Python, ESP32 & IoT | Data Logging, AI, and Cloud Connectivity |

### 🌸 Climate Tech Project (Ages 12–14)
A specialized program designed to encourage young women to lead in the fields of STEM and Climate Science.
